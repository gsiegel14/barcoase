## 📊 **Distribution Methods**

### **High-Impact Methods:**
1. **Department Head Announcement** - Ask department heads to share in team meetings
2. **Break Room Posters** - QR codes in high-traffic areas
3. **Email Signature** - Add to email signatures
4. **Badge Buddy Cards** - Small cards that clip to ID badges

### **Digital Distribution:**
1. **Internal Chat/Slack** - Share in department channels
2. **Hospital Newsletter** - Include in next newsletter
3. **Intranet Announcement** - Post on hospital intranet
4. **Staff Facebook Groups** - Share in private staff groups

### **Word-of-Mouth:**
1. **Champion Network** - Train 2-3 tech-savvy staff per department
2. **Buddy System** - Staff help colleagues install
3. **Demonstration** - Show it working during shift changes

No analytics in the app (privacy first), but you can:
- Ask department heads for informal feedback
- Monitor support requests
- Survey staff in a few weeks
- Watch for reduction in manual QR processing

## 🆘 **Support Preparation**

**Common Questions:**
- "Is this approved by IT?" → It's just a website, no IT approval needed
- "Is my data safe?" → Everything stays on your device, nothing transmitted
- "What if it breaks?" → Just reinstall from the same URL
- "Can I delete it?" → Yes, delete like any other app

**Troubleshooting:**
- App won't install → Try different browser (Chrome/Safari)
- Camera not working → Check browser permissions
- Not working offline → Make sure you opened it once while online
